What every tune-up covers

  • Lubricate rollers, hinges, bearings and springs
  • Tighten all hardware, brackets and track bolts
  • Check spring tension and door balance
  • Inspect lift cables for fraying
  • Check rollers and tracks for wear and alignment
  • Inspect weather seals and bottom rubber
  • Test and align safety sensors
  • Test auto-reverse safety function
  • Check opener force and travel settings
  • Test remotes and wall button
  • Full open-and-close run test
  • Written report with photos of anything worn
